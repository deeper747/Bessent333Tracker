The 3-3-3 Tracker

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent set three goals for 2028: 3 percent real growth, a deficit of 3 percent of GDP, and 3 million more barrels of oil equivalent a day. This page measures each against public data.

Sources. Real GDP: Bureau of Economic Analysis via FRED. Deficit: Monthly Treasury Statement via FRED, divided by nominal GDP. Energy: Energy Information Administration monthly crude oil, natural gas plant liquids, and dry natural gas production.

Method. The deficit is the sum of the latest 12 monthly balances over the most recent quarterly nominal GDP. Natural gas converts at 6 thousand cubic feet per barrel of oil equivalent. The energy baseline is the 2024 average; the deficit baseline is January 2025.
